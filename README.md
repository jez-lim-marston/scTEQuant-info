# scTEQuant

**Bayesian quantification of viral and transposable-element expression from RNA-seq**

scTEQuant is a Bayesian pipeline for locus-specific quantification of viral and
transposable-element transcription from single-cell, bulk, and spatial RNA-seq.
It is being developed for studies of how viruses and virus-derived elements
shape immune cell function, including EBV and HIV-1 in lymphoma.
